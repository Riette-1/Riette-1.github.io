Read for a minute or more and your pace will appear here.

Real pace is the words you actually read per minute, pauses at punctuation included, so it runs a little below your speed setting.

Flash

One word at a time in a fixed spot, so your eyes never have to travel. The red letter marks where your eye naturally locks on.

Pacer

A highlight sweeps through the page at your speed, like running a finger under the line, so you don't drift back.

Smart timing

Slightly longer pauses at commas, sentence ends, paragraphs and long words keep comprehension up.
