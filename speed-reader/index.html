Flash (RSVP)

Rapid Serial Visual Presentation shows one word (or a small chunk) at a time in a fixed spot, so your eyes stop jumping across the line. The red letter is the optimal recognition point, where your eye naturally locks on.

Pacer

A highlight sweeps through the page at your target speed, like running a finger under the text. It keeps you moving forward and discourages re-reading (regression).

Smart timing & Bionic

Pauses lengthen at commas, sentence ends, paragraphs and long words so comprehension holds up. Bionic mode bolds the start of each word to guide fixation in Pacer view.
